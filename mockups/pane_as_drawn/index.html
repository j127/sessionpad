sessionpad pane, as the mod draws it — design mockups

Three directions for the redesigned pane, each drawn only with what the mod can draw on Desktop (light and dark) and in a terminal, as measured in sessionpad_pane/RENDERING.md. Open a mockup and use its design panel to explore modes, palettes, fonts, radius, and density.

01 · Pills

The open tab as a black pill among grey ones, a headline card in the accent, notes on grey shading with pill buttons, an inline undo, and the timeline as shaded day groups.

02 · Cards

Soft rounded cards everywhere: the headline, each note, each timeline day and the workspace sit on filled grey cards, with the open tab a black pill beside plain-text tabs and an undo card at the top.

03 · Ledger

Dense and aligned: monospace throughout, striped rows in fixed columns, compact H E X pills with a key line, a Recently deleted shelf, and a tinted headline strip.
